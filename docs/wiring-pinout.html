IRXBot — Wiring Pinout

Jetson Orin Nano · STM32 NUCLEO-F446RE · ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1  |  Taken from the firmware on main, 2026-11-02

Voltage colours: 3.3 V · 5 V · 12 V · 24 V · GND.

Before power-on: (1) All boards share one common GND. (2) STM32 and ESP32 signal pins are 3.3 V; never put 5 V, 12 V or 24 V on a signal pin. (3) CAN: about 60 Ω between CAN-H and CAN-L with power off. (4) The E-stop must cut the contactor — test it last.

2. STM32 NUCLEO-F446RE

"Header" = the Arduino-style header name printed on the Nucleo. Pins with no header name are on the long morpho headers (CN7 / CN10). Do not wire PA2 / PA3 (D1 / D0): they carry the USB link to the Jetson. The ToF pin labels in CubeMX (TOF_XSHUT_FRONT etc.) are out of date; this table follows the firmware.
